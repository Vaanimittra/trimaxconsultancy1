Buyer's Guide

Essential steps to help you navigate the luxury property buying process.

📝 Step 1: Define Your Vision

Identify your requirements — budget, location, size, amenities, and purpose (weekend home, investment, or primary residence).

🔍 Step 2: Research & Shortlist

Explore properties, compare options, and shortlist the ones that match your criteria. Consider factors like connectivity and future development.

🏗️ Step 3: Site Visit & Inspection

Visit the property in person. Inspect the construction quality, surrounding area, and amenities. Ask about legal clearances and title deeds.

💰 Step 4: Financial Planning

Evaluate your budget, explore home loan options, and calculate additional costs like registration fees, taxes, and maintenance charges.

📄 Step 5: Legal Verification

Verify property documents, title deeds, and approvals. Ensure the property is free from legal disputes and encumbrances.

🤝 Step 6: Closing the Deal

Finalize the agreement, complete the payment process, and register the property. Get possession and enjoy your new home!
